## Restoration emergencies common in Burbank

Burbank sits in southwest Cook County, where flat terrain, aging storm sewer infrastructure, and a mix of mid-century housing stock combine to produce a predictable set of problems. Summer thunderstorms that stall over the Chicago area can overwhelm combined and storm sewers faster than they drain, pushing water back through floor drains and sump systems into basements and crawlspaces. Winter brings its own version of the same problem: frozen or cracked supply lines in homes with minimal attic or crawlspace insulation, and ice damming on pitched asphalt-shingle roofs that forces melt water under shingles and into ceiling cavities. Between freeze-thaw cycles and sewer backup risk, water intrusion here is rarely a single clean event, it tends to show up in more than one place at once.

## Services we provide in Burbank

Water damage in Burbank often starts in a basement or crawlspace after a sewer backup or sump failure, then spreads upward through wood subfloor and drywall before anyone notices the smell. Our water removal and structural drying work follows the IICRC S500 standard, with moisture mapping to confirm materials are actually dry, not just dry at the surface.

Mold remediation becomes relevant fast in this area because basement moisture that goes unaddressed for even a few days can colonize framing and insulation. We contain the affected area, remove compromised materials, and address the moisture source so the problem does not return after cosmetic repairs.

Storm damage response covers wind, hail, and flooding impacts to roofing, siding, and foundations after the thunderstorm systems that regularly move through the region. Biohazard cleanup, including sewage backup and trauma scenes, is handled with the containment and disposal protocols the work requires.
