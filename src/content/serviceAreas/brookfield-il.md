## Restoration emergencies common in Brookfield

Brookfield sits along Salt Creek, and the village has a long, well-documented history of creek flooding during heavy spring rain and fast snowmelt. Basements and crawlspaces in low-lying sections of town take on water even when the storm itself has passed, because the creek crests hours after the rain stops. Add in a winter freeze-thaw cycle that stresses older supply lines, and the result is a steady mix of sump pump failures, frozen and burst pipes, and seepage through aging foundation walls. Housing stock built before mid-century also means more properties with original plumbing runs and roof assemblies that weren't designed for today's storm intensity, which is part of why water intrusion here tends to show up in places owners don't expect.
