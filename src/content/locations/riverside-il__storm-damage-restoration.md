## Why Riverside Properties See Storm Damage

Riverside sits directly along the Des Plaines River, and the village's historic landscape design preserved large stands of mature oaks and elms throughout residential blocks. That's a beautiful feature most of the year, but during a severe thunderstorm or straight-line wind event, those older trees are the first thing to come down, often onto roofs, detached garages, or power lines. Add to that a housing stock heavy with homes built in the early-to-mid 1900s, many with original wood-frame roof decking and older gutter systems, and you get a pattern where a single storm produces both impact damage from falling limbs and secondary water intrusion once a roof is compromised. The river's floodplain also means some low-lying blocks see backyard and basement flooding even when the wind damage itself is minor.

## Our Storm Damage Restoration Process in Riverside

The first visit is about stopping further loss, not full repair. We tarp exposed roof sections, board broken windows, and extract any standing water before it reaches subfloor or plaster walls that are common in Riverside's older homes. Once the property is secured, we assess structural framing, check attic insulation for saturation, and document everything with photos your adjuster will need. Drying follows the IICRC S500 standard for structural drying, with extra attention paid to older lath-and-plaster sections, which hold moisture longer than modern drywall and require slower, monitored drying to avoid cracking or delamination. Tree and debris removal is coordinated separately from the water mitigation work so nothing gets rushed past what the insurance claim requires.

## Riverside Insurance & HOA Coordination

Most homeowners' policies cover wind and storm-driven water damage, but coverage details shift depending on whether the damage is classified as wind, flood, or backup, especially for homes closer to the river where basement water intrusion can get questioned by adjusters as a flood exclusion rather than storm damage. We photograph and log the sequence of damage, wind first, then water, so the claim reflects what actually happened on your property. If your block falls under a historic district designation, which several Riverside areas do given the village's Olmsted-era planning, we also flag any exterior repair work that may need to match original materials or go through additional village review before permits are finalized.
