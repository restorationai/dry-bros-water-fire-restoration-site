## Why Burbank Properties See Sewage Backup Issues

Burbank's post-war bungalows and ranch homes were largely built with clay or cast-iron sewer laterals that are now decades past their original service life. Add in Cook County's pattern of intense summer storms and a municipal system that, like much of the Chicago area, relies on combined sewer infrastructure in older sections, and you get a straightforward mechanism: stormwater overwhelms capacity, and the path of least resistance is back up through the lowest drain in the house. Flat terrain across the southwest suburbs means water doesn't drain away from foundations quickly, so a slow lateral clog or a municipal surcharge can sit against your home's connection point for hours before anyone notices the smell or the puddle at the floor drain.

## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process

Sewage work starts with containment, not demolition. We isolate the affected area to stop cross-contamination into HVAC returns or adjoining rooms, then extract standing wastewater with pumps rated for solids-laden liquid, not standard water-damage equipment. Porous materials that contacted raw sewage, carpet padding, baseboard, drywall below the flood line, generally can't be saved; we remove them per category 3 water guidelines rather than attempt to dry and treat them. Once the space is stripped to sound material, we apply EPA-registered antimicrobial treatment, then run air movers and dehumidifiers set to the room's actual moisture load rather than a fixed schedule. We verify dryness with moisture meters before any rebuild conversation starts, because closing up a wall over damp framing is how call-backs happen.
