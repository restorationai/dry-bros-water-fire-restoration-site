## Our Water Damage Restoration Process in Stickney

Every call starts with identifying the water category and extracting standing water with truck-mounted or portable extraction equipment, since pooled water left sitting on hardwood, tile, or carpet pad accelerates both material damage and microbial growth. From there we map moisture in framing, subfloor, and drywall with meters and thermal imaging, because water travels along studs and under baseboards well past where it looks wet on the surface. Structural drying follows the IICRC S500 standard, with air movers and dehumidifiers placed and monitored daily until materials read dry, not just dry to the touch. In homes with finished basements, that often means pulling baseboards or cutting small access points in drywall rather than tearing out entire walls, which keeps the repair scope and the insurance claim smaller.
