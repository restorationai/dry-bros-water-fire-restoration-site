## Why Lyons Properties See Water Damage Issues

Lyons is one of the older inner-ring Cook County suburbs, and a lot of the housing stock here, bungalows and two-flats built in the mid-20th century, was constructed with clay tile foundations and cast iron plumbing that's long past its expected service life. Combine that with the village's proximity to the Des Plaines River and the area's naturally high water table, and you get basements that take on water not just from storms but from groundwater seepage during wet stretches. Older sewer laterals in this part of Cook County also back up during heavy rain events, which means a water loss here is frequently a mixed problem: part storm intrusion, part aging infrastructure, and sometimes part sewage, which changes how we classify and handle the water from the start.

## Our Water Damage Restoration Process in Lyons

We start every call with a moisture assessment, checking not just the visible wet flooring but what's happening inside the wall cavities and under subfloors, because water in a Lyons basement often travels further than it looks like it has. Extraction comes next, pulling standing water out of carpet, pad, and any finished basement materials before it has a chance to wick upward into drywall. From there we set up structural drying equipment calibrated to the specific moisture load, dense air movers and dehumidifiers sized for the room, and we track drying progress with moisture meters rather than guessing based on how a surface feels. Homes with the clay tile and rubble foundations common in this part of Lyons tend to hold moisture in the foundation wall itself longer than poured concrete, so we extend monitoring in those cases rather than closing out the job on a fixed schedule.
