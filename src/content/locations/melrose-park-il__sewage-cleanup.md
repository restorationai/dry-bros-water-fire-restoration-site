Sewage cleanup and sanitization isn't a job to delay: contaminated water carries bacteria and pathogens that spread fast through drywall, subfloor, and ductwork, and in a lot of Melrose Park's older housing stock, that spread happens quicker than homeowners expect because of how these homes were originally plumbed.

## Why Melrose Park Properties See Sewage Backups

Melrose Park sits in Proviso Township, an inner-ring Chicago suburb with a lot of housing built in the early-to-mid 1900s, when clay tile sewer laterals were standard. Those lines are now seventy to ninety years old in many cases, and clay tile is prone to root intrusion, joint separation, and collapse long before anyone notices a slow drain turning into a full backup. Add in the heavy spring and summer storms common across Cook County, and aging municipal lines that weren't sized for today's rainfall volumes, and you get a pattern we see often here: sewage pushing back through basement floor drains and laundry hookups during or right after a storm event. Flat-lying lots and older basement construction mean that water and waste tend to collect rather than drain away on their own, which is exactly why fast extraction matters.

## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process

We treat every sewage call as a Category 3 loss, meaning the water is grossly contaminated and every porous material it touched, carpet, pad, drywall, baseboards, often needs to come out rather than be dried in place. The process starts with containment to stop cross-contamination into unaffected rooms, followed by extraction of standing water and waste, removal of saturated porous materials, and cleaning of structural surfaces and contents with EPA-registered antimicrobial products. We dry the space per IICRC S500 guidelines, using air movers and dehumidifiers sized to the room, and we verify dryness with moisture meters before closing anything back up. In homes with older cast iron or clay lateral lines, we also recommend a camera inspection of the sewer line itself. Cleaning up the mess without identifying why it happened just invites a repeat call.

## Local note

A lot of Melrose Park's bungalow and cape cod-style homes have their primary floor drain positioned at the lowest point of an unfinished basement, often right next to the furnace or water heater. When sewage backs up, it tends to pool around those mechanicals first, which means an HVAC or water heater inspection is usually part of the job, not an afterthought. We check those units for contamination before anyone turns the furnace back on.
