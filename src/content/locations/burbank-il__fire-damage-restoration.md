## Why Burbank Homes Face Added Fire Risk

Burbank's housing stock is dominated by brick bungalows and ranch homes built in the postwar decades, many still running original or upgraded-once electrical panels that weren't designed for today's appliance load. Combine that with Chicago-area winters, where space heaters and older forced-air furnaces run hard for months at a time, and you get a steady stream of heating-related fire calls every cold season. Attached garages, common throughout the area, also mean a garage fire can spread into living space faster than homeowners expect, since the shared wall is often a single layer of drywall over wood framing rather than a fire-rated assembly.

Smoke behaves differently in these older brick homes too. Soot works into plaster and lath faster than it does into modern drywall, and it travels through forced-air ductwork into rooms nowhere near the original fire. A kitchen fire can leave bedrooms upstairs smelling like smoke within hours, simply because the HVAC system pulled particulates through the return vents while it was still running.

## Our Fire Damage Restoration Process

We start with board-up and tarping to secure the structure, then move into soot and residue removal, working from the point of origin outward. Porous materials (drywall, insulation, carpet padding) that have absorbed smoke odor usually can't be deodorized back to normal and get removed rather than treated. Framing and subfloor that are structurally sound but smoke-stained get cleaned with the appropriate chemical sponge or wet-cleaning method depending on the soot type, dry or wet, protein-based or synthetic.

Once the structure is clean, we run air scrubbers and thermal fogging to address lingering odor in the HVAC system and wall cavities, since smoke odor that's only masked tends to come back with humidity changes. Drying runs alongside this work whenever firefighting water has soaked into subfloors or framing, using the same moisture-mapping approach we'd use on a water loss, because a fire-damaged structure that stays wet is just setting up a second problem behind the first.
