## Our Water Damage Restoration Process in Melrose Park

The first visit is about finding every place water has traveled, not just where it's visible. In homes with plaster walls or dense old-growth subflooring, water extraction happens fast on the surface, but the material underneath holds moisture far longer than modern drywall and plywood, so drying plans get built around the actual materials in the house rather than a generic timeline. We extract standing water, pull affected baseboard or flooring where needed to check for trapped moisture, and set structural drying equipment calibrated to the square footage and the humidity load in the space. Moisture readings get logged at the start and checked daily so drying stays data-driven instead of guesswork, and drying per the IICRC S500 standard means the structure isn't called dry until the readings confirm it, not just the surface.

## Melrose Park Insurance Coordination

Most Melrose Park water losses we see fall into a few categories carriers recognize clearly: sudden pipe failure, sump pump or sewer backup, and storm-driven basement intrusion. We document the loss with photos and moisture readings as we go, which gives your adjuster a clear record of what was affected and what work was done, and we bill most major carriers directly so you're not covering the full cost upfront while the claim is reviewed. Basement sewer backup claims sometimes fall under a separate endorsement than standard water damage coverage, so it's worth checking your policy or asking your agent before water hits the floor, not after.
